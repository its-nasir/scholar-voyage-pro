# Dream Scholars Portal

Build a Professional Study Abroad Consultancy Website

Create a modern, premium, trustworthy and fully responsive website for an overseas education consultancy company named:

Dearm Scholars Abroad Pvt. Ltd.

Company Founder & Director

Md Tahir Hussain

The website should position Dearm Scholars Abroad Pvt. Ltd. as a professional education consultancy that helps students study abroad and assists them in finding suitable universities, courses, scholarships and admission opportunities.

The overall website should feel premium, trustworthy, student-friendly, international and professional.

1. Website Goal

The primary purpose of the website is to:

Help students explore study-abroad opportunities.

Help students find suitable countries, universities and courses.

Promote scholarship opportunities.

Generate student enquiries and leads.

Allow students to request counselling.

Explain the complete admission process.

Build trust in the consultancy.

Showcase the company's expertise and services.

Provide easy contact options through phone, email and WhatsApp.

Encourage students to book a free counselling session.

The website should be designed primarily for students and parents who are planning higher education abroad.
